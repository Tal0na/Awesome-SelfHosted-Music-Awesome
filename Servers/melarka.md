# 🎧 Melarka

Self-hosted music server for a household, with its own web app and native iPhone and Android apps.

---

## ⭐ Features

- **Support:** its own web, iPhone and Android apps (not Subsonic-compatible)
- **License:** AGPL-3.0
- **Open Source:** ✅ Yes
- **Price:** 🆓 Free
- **Last Update:** 🟢 Actively maintained
- **Highlights:**
  - One Docker container (Go + SQLite), one-command install
  - Reads your music folders and never rewrites files or tags; title, tag and lyrics fixes live in its own database
  - Can also add songs from YouTube (search, playlists, followed channels via yt-dlp)
  - One account per family member: own favorites, playlists, personal radio, nightly picks
  - Synced lyrics; anyone can shift lyrics that run early or late
  - Streams the original file or transcodes to AAC on the fly
  - Native apps: background play, lock-screen and car controls, gapless, offline cache of favorites, autoplay when the phone connects to the car over Bluetooth (with the current lyric line on the car display)
  - Interface in English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Swedish
